Adding someone

  1. On the server: ./deploy/add-user.sh alice — this prints a single-use invite link.
  2. Send them the link. They choose their own password in the browser.
  3. Send them the three values above. They paste them into Claude's connector settings.
  4. They sign in here and click Approve.

Nothing in that list needs a terminal on their side. To cut someone off later, disable their account with ./deploy/add-user.sh disable alice — deleting the connector here would disconnect everybody.
